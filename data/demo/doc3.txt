1. PURPOSE AND USAGE

1.1 This Directory identifies designated Subject Matter Experts for advisory consultations, design reviews, policy interpretation, and incident escalation. It does NOT replace ServiceNow. Operational requests (access, firewall changes, HR transactions) must be submitted through the appropriate ServiceNow catalog item.

1.2 Consultation channels: the "Ask an Expert" ServiceNow form (preferred), Slack direct message during posted office hours, or PagerDuty where the SME holds an escalation rotation.

1.3 Consultation Response SLA: Standard: 2 business days. Urgent (SEV1/SEV2 incident, regulatory deadline, or active legal matter): same business day, or via PagerDuty where applicable.

1.4 Proficiency Scale:
    L5 – Authority:     Recognized internal authority; sets standards; final technical escalation point.
    L4 – Expert:        Deep expertise; leads complex designs and reviews independently.
    L3 – Advanced:      Strong working knowledge; handles most scenarios without assistance.
    L2 – Practitioner:  Working knowledge; handles routine scenarios.
    L1 – Foundational:  Awareness-level knowledge.
