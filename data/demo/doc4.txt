1. PURPOSE

1.1 This Policy establishes the mandatory standards, timelines, and accountabilities governing the onboarding of all new employees of Corvanta Technologies, Inc. ("Corvanta" or "the Company"). It ensures every new hire is legally compliant, securely provisioned, and productive within the first ninety (90) calendar days of employment.

1.2 Objectives:
    (a) Satisfy employment eligibility, tax, and data privacy obligations in every jurisdiction in which Corvanta operates.
    (b) Ensure no new hire accesses Confidential (C2) or Restricted (C3) information prior to completing mandatory security training.
    (c) Reduce median time-to-productivity to 60 calendar days (FY2025 baseline: 74 days).
    (d) Deliver a consistent, measurable onboarding experience (target 90-day new-hire eNPS of +40 or higher).

2. SCOPE

2.1 This Policy applies to: (a) full-time regular employees; (b) part-time regular employees scheduled for 20 or more hours per week; (c) fixed-term employees with an initial contract of six (6) months or longer; (d) rehires with a break in service exceeding 90 calendar days; and (e) interns engaged for 10 weeks or longer (Sections 5, 7, and 10 only).

2.2 Contractors, consultants, and contingent workers are governed by VMP-POL-210 (Contingent Workforce Policy). HOWEVER, Section 7 (Mandatory Security & Compliance Training) applies to every individual issued a Corvanta network account, without exception.

2.3 Internal transfers are subject only to Section 7.3 (Role-Based Training) and the access validation requirement in Checklist Item 2.03.

3. DEFINITIONS

- Start Date: The employee's first day of active employment, counted as "Day 1" and "Business Day 1 (BD 1)."
- Day n: The nth calendar day of employment, counting the Start Date as Day 1.
- BD n: The nth business day of employment (Monday–Friday, excluding Company holidays observed at the employee's work location), counting the Start Date as BD 1.
- Hiring Manager: The people leader to whom the new hire directly reports.
- Onboarding Buddy: A tenured peer (minimum 12 months of service and a "Meets Expectations" or higher rating in the most recent review cycle) assigned by the Hiring Manager.
- HRBP: HR Business Partner assigned to the employee's business unit.
- POSC: People Operations Service Center — hr-help@corvanta.com, ext. 4357 (HELP), or the ServiceNow HR Service Portal.
- Workday: Corvanta's Human Resources Information System (HRIS) and system of record for employee data.
- Corvanta Learn: The Company's enterprise Learning Management System (LMS).
- RBAP: Role-Based Access Profile — the standard set of system entitlements assigned to a job code.
- Probationary Period: The first 90 calendar days of employment (see Section 9).

4. PRE-BOARDING (OFFER ACCEPTANCE THROUGH DAY -1)

4.1 Background Screening. All offers are contingent upon satisfactory completion of a background check through the Company's approved screening vendor. Screening must be completed no later than three (3) business days before the Start Date. No Start Date may be confirmed in Workday until screening status is "Cleared."

4.2 Start Date Cohorts. Start Dates are scheduled on Mondays only (Tuesday when Monday is a Company holidays). Exceptions require HRBP approval.

4.3 Provisioning Request. The Hiring Manager MUST submit the "New Hire Provisioning Request" (ServiceNow catalog item NHPR-01) no later than ten (10) business days before the Start Date, specifying the RBAP, hardware profile, software, distribution lists, and building access. Requests submitted late are not guaranteed for Day 1 fulfillment.

4.4 Identity Data Collection. New hires submit personal data, emergency contacts, tax forms (US: Form W-4 and state equivalents; Ireland: PPSN registration with Revenue; India: PAN and UAN for EPF), and direct deposit details ONLY through the Workday Pre-Hire Portal. In accordance with Policy SEC-04 (Rules PII-03 and PII-12), recruiters, coordinators, and managers must NEVER request government identification numbers, bank details, or images of identity documents by email, chat, or text message. Any such request must be reported to the Security Operations Center (security@corvanta.com).

4.5 Account Creation. The IT Service Desk creates Entra ID and Okta accounts in a disabled state on Day -3. Accounts are activated at 07:00 local time on Day 1 upon People Operations' confirmation of identity verification.

4.6 Buddy Assignment. The Hiring Manager assigns an Onboarding Buddy in Workday no later than Day -5.

7.4 Phishing Simulation. Simulations are conducted monthly. A new hire who fails two (2) simulations within the first 90 days must complete remedial course SEC-103R within seven (7) calendar days. A third failure is reported to the Hiring Manager and HRBP.

7.5 Non-Compliance Escalation (measured from the missed deadline):
    - +1 business day:   Automated reminder to employee and Hiring Manager.
    - +3 business days:  Escalation to the Hiring Manager's manager (skip-level); item flagged in Workday.
    - +5 business days:  IT suspends access to all non-essential applications. Email, Workday, Corvanta Learn, and the Okta dashboard remain available. Suspension lifts automatically upon course completion.
    - +10 business days: Escalation to the HRBP and the Office of the CISO; documented as a performance concern; may preclude a "Confirm" probationary outcome (Section 9.3). Recorded as a V3 (Medium) violation under SEC-04 Section 12.

7.6 Annual Refresher. SEC-101, SEC-102, SEC-103, and PRV-201 must be recompleted annually by January 31. Employees with a Start Date on or after October 1 are exempt from the immediately following January cycle.

7.7 Deadline Extensions. Granted only for an approved leave of absence. The extension equals the duration of the leave plus five (5) business days and is requested via ServiceNow HR case. Extensions never waive access gates in Sections 7.2 and 7.3.

8. PAID TIME OFF, HOLIDAYS, AND LEAVE

8.1 PTO Accrual (United States):

Completed Years of Service | Annual PTO           | Accrual per Semi-Monthly Pay Period | Maximum Accrual Cap
---------------------------|----------------------|-------------------------------------|--------------------
0–2 years                  | 15 days (120 hours)  | 5.00 hours                          | 180 hours
3–5 years                  | 20 days (160 hours)  | 6.67 hours                          | 240 hours
6–9 years                  | 23 days (184 hours)  | 7.67 hours                          | 276 hours
10+ years                  | 25 days (200 hours)  | 8.33 hours                          | 300 hours

Accrual stops when the cap is reached and resumes once the balance falls below the cap.

8.2 New Hire PTO Rules:
    (a) PTO accrual begins on the Start Date and is prorated for partial pay periods.
    (b) Accrued PTO may be used only after completion of 30 calendar days of employment.
    (c) Exception: pre-hire commitments disclosed in writing before offer acceptance may be honored with HRBP approval (maximum 5 days). This may create a negative balance of up to -40 hours, recoverable from final pay where permitted by law.
    (d) No PTO may be scheduled during the first five (5) business days except for documented emergencies.

8.3 Carryover. Up to 40 hours (5 days) of unused PTO carries over into the next calendar year and must be used by March 31 or it is forfeited. Where state law prohibits forfeiture (e.g., California, Colorado, Montana, Nebraska), accrued PTO is not forfeited, but the accrual cap in Section 8.1 applies.

8.4 Requesting PTO. All PTO is requested in Workday. Absences of 3 or more consecutive business days require at least 10 business days' notice. Shorter absences require at least 2 business days' notice. Managers must respond within 3 business days.
    Blackout periods:
    - Finance & Accounting: the last 5 and first 5 business days of each fiscal quarter close.
    - Infrastructure Operations & Network Engineering: the annual Peak Change Freeze (Monday before US Thanksgiving through the following Friday), except with Director approval.

8.5 Paid Holidays (US). Eleven (11) fixed holidays: New Year's Day, Martin Luther King Jr. Day, Presidents' Day, Memorial Day, Juneteenth, Independence Day, Labor Day, Thanksgiving Day, Day after Thanksgiving, Christmas Eve, and Christmas Day. In addition, two (2) floating holidays are granted each January 1. New hires starting after June 30 receive one (1) floating holiday. Floating holidays do not carry over.

8.6 Sick Leave (US). Ten (10) days (80 hours) granted on January 1 each year, prorated for new hires, and available from Day 1. Sick leave does not carry over and is not paid out at separation. Where local law is more generous, local law prevails.

8.7 Other Leave Types:
    - Bereavement: 5 days for immediate family; 3 days for extended family.
    - Parental Leave: 16 weeks at 100% of base pay for birth, adoptive, and foster parents. Eligibility begins after 90 days of continuous service. Leave must be taken within 12 months of birth or placement and runs concurrently with FMLA where applicable.
    - FMLA: Eligible after 12 months of service and 1,250 hours worked in the preceding 12 months.
    - Jury Duty: Full pay for up to 15 business days per calendar year.
    - Volunteer Time Off (VTO): 2 days (16 hours) per calendar year.
    - Military Leave: In accordance with USERRA.

9. PROBATIONARY PERIOD

9.1 The Probationary Period is the first 90 calendar days of employment. For US employees, employment remains at-will during and after the Probationary Period. For Ireland and India, probation length and notice terms are defined in the employment contract (both regions: 6 months). The 30-60-90 checklist in Section 6 applies in all regions.

9.2 The Hiring Manager must complete the Probationary Review in Workday by Day 85 with one of the following outcomes:
    (a) Confirm — the employee has met expectations.
    (b) Extend — permitted once, for a maximum of 30 calendar days. Requires HRBP approval and documented improvement objectives.
    (c) Separate — requires HRBP and Employee Relations review before communication to the employee.

9.3 An outcome of "Confirm" may not be recorded while any mandatory item 1.01–1.16, or any Section 7.2 core course, is incomplete. The outcome remains pending until remediated.

10. ROLES AND RESPONSIBILITIES

- New Employee: Completes all assigned tasks and training by deadline; raises blockers to the Hiring Manager or POSC.
- Hiring Manager: Submits NHPR-01 on time; owns the 30-60-90 plan, check-ins, access validation, and Probationary Review.
- Onboarding Buddy: Provides cultural and practical guidance; conducts weekly check-ins through Day 30 and is available through Day 90.
- People Operations: Administers pre-boarding, NHO, identity verification, the Workday Onboarding Journey, and reporting.
- HRBP: Approves exceptions and probationary outcomes; advises managers.
- IT Service Desk: Provisions accounts, devices, and RBAP entitlements; enforces training-based access gates.
- Office of the CISO / Security Awareness: Owns SEC-series curricula and phishing simulations; receives Section 7.5 escalations.

11. EXCEPTIONS

Exceptions to this Policy are requested via ServiceNow HR case type "Onboarding Policy Exception" and require HRBP approval. Exceptions affecting Section 7 also require approval from the Office of the CISO. No exception may waive the access gates in Sections 7.2 and 7.3. Approved exceptions are retained with the employee record for 7 years following separation.
